### The project
We are advertising a fully paid, 2-year postdoctoral researcher position at Aalto University’s School of Science, Department of Computer Science, to work on the project “Autotelic Creative Artificial Intelligence” (ACAI).

Critical at its core, the ACAI project seeks to identify alternative and sustainable paths to “mainstream” creative AI. Rather than optimizing systems to replicate established human aesthetics or processes, we investigate the conditions under which AI might operate as a genuinely creative agent. Amongst other questions, we ask: Can AI be creative in its own right, rather than emulating human creativity? Can such AI be autotelic, i.e., define its own goals and methods of inquiry? What forms of autotelic creativity exist in nature, and might we miss them given our situatedness?

To further these questions, we will rely on the candidate to study the foundations of creativity in AI and living beings, including e.g. agency, motivation/teleology and value grounding.

Crucially, this project seeks to combine such theoretic research with the critical artistic practice of a media art PhD researcher. Through regular interaction and individual exploration, the theoretical research will inspire the artistic practice to articulate our findings in speculative AI futures, providing the basis for wider engagement with other theorists, creatives and the wider public (via exhibitions, art commissions, and public engagement). Vice versa, the successful candidate will be expected to engage with art and artists to support their research process, which presents the challenge and opportunity to engage with very different expertise and epistemologies.

Through this research programme, ACAI aims to critically assess both the positively and negatively transformative societal implications of creative AI, while contributing new theoretical, artistic, and technical frameworks for understanding creativity beyond the human realm. The focus is not on shaping AI research through insights from its present use in artistic practice, but on advancing publications and evocative speculative futures to further critical engagement in different communities within academia and beyond. Artists are considered as equal contributors to the research.

### The position
We seek a creative and open-minded postdoctoral researcher who is intellectually and technologically curious, disciplinarily promiscuous, investigative in their research and who enjoys convening and dialoguing across genres led by their (post)humanist values.

We welcome submissions from applicants from a diverse range of disciplines such as cogsci, computer science, philosophy, psychology, and theoretical biology (not exhaustive and in no particular order) who can demonstrate (also via publications) an interest in AI and creativity, a critical mind, the ability to work formally and with theory, and expertise with one or several of the following concepts: machine creativity, agency, autonomy, motivation/teleology, value grounding and open-endedness. If in doubt, please contact us with your profile prior to submission.

The ideal candidate is a reflective thinker with the ability and motivation to:
1. Survey and translate between research on a wide range of relevant concepts (above).
2. Engage with different types of research, including e.g. empirical studies from Psychology on animal creativity and AI papers on formal accounts of agency (examples below).
3. Communicate their insights at an appropriate level of abstraction to the artistic member of the team and, vice versa, engage with the artistic work to inform their research.

A strong application will support each point by reference to past activities. Next to research synthesis, ACAI has space for the candidate to advance specific research on any of the involved concepts. Next to contributing to ACAI’s milestones, the postdoc will be given time to pursue their own projects which should nonetheless link to the group’s interests (see website). Throughout the project, they will be interacting closely with the artist and supervisor.

### Location, duties & compensation
Aalto is located in the Helsinki metropolitan area, Finland. The applicant is expected to relocate.

The position is for 2 years and compensated with 4229 EUR/month , which is complemented with an annual holiday bonus and occupational healthcare benefits.

The candidate is expected to dedicate at least 5% of their time to teaching (e.g. lectures, teaching support, supervision of theses), matched with their individual skills and research. Moreover, they are expected to engage in overseas travel (e.g. research collaborations and conferences).

For their net income, candidates can check out vero.fi calculators. For an overview of living costs in Finland and the Helsinki metropolitan area, see https://infofinland.fi/settling-in-finland.
