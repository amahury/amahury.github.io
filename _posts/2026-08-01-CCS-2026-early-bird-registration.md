### General Information
The early-bird registration deadline for the [2026 Conference on Complex Systems](https://ccs26.cssociety.org/) (CCS 2026) is August 20, 2026.

The event is the flagship international gathering hosted by the [Complex Systems Society](https://cssociety.org/event/5ea1ef85-35de-4e54-9a0c-772f4dcd0185/). It takes place from October 9 to October 16, 2026, on the main campus of Binghamton University in Binghamton, New York, with a virtual attendance option available via Zoom.

### Conference Timeline
* August 20, 2026: Early-bird registration deadline.
* September 20, 2026: Final registration deadline for presenters.
* October 9–10, 2026: Young Researchers of the Complex Systems Society (yrCSS) warm-up events.
* October 12–16, 2026: Main conference and exhibitions. 

### Registration Fees & Categories
All tier registrations include a one-year Complex Systems Society membership fee, and full conference passes include a ticket to the conference dinner.

| Ticket Category | Early-Bird Fee (Until Aug 20) | Regular Fee (After Aug 20) |
|---|---|---|
| Student (Full Conference) | $300 | $400 |
| Postdoc (Full Conference) | $400 | $500 |
| Academic (Full Conference) | $500 | $600 |
| Non-Academic (Full Conference) | $600 | $700 |
| Online Only | $150 | $250 |

Source and registration: [https://ccs26.cssociety.org/registration.html](https://ccs26.cssociety.org/registration.html)
