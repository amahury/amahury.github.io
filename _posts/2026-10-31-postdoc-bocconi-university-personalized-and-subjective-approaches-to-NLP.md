### Research Project
PERSONAE will make language technology (LT) accessible and valuable to everyone. It will
revolutionize research in subjective tasks in NLP such as abusive language detection and
sentiment and emotion analysis by developing a new field called personal NLP, yielding
new datasets, tasks, and algorithms. This new research area will explore subjective tasks
from the perspective of the individual as information receiver, making users active actors
in the creation of LTs instead of mere recipients. This will allow for a more tailored,
effective approach to NLP model design, resulting in better models overall. Each person has
their own interests and preferences based on their background and experience. These
factors impact their views of what makes them happy, angry, or depressed over time.
Language technologies (LTs) can consider individual preferences. However, current
research presumes a static view of subjectivity: that a single ground truth underlies
subjective tasks such as abusive language detection, an assumption that lacks human
variability and prevents universal access to LTs. Language-based AI such as virtual 
assistants is widely available. But despite significant scientific advances, most LT
applications are inaccessible to individuals and their public's opinion has become
increasingly negative. GPT-3's 2020 release boosted business-oriented applications such as
copywriting and chatbots, yet few that let people improve their lives—for example, by
controlling what they see on social media. This gap becomes more pronounced for
subjective tasks. PERSONAE will design subjective LTs that can be adapted by individuals
at will over time. Based on an ambitious meta approach able to generalize from existing,
disconnected work, PERSONAE will rely on fully personalizable privacy-aware algorithms
that can be used by anyone. It will reveal benefits of LT far beyond those of existing
systems, paving the way for future applications.

### Responsibilities in the Project
Successful candidates will actively collaborate with the research team to develop and
implement new algorithms and methodologies in the field of Natural Language Processing
(NLP). Primary responsibilities include participating in the design and execution of
experiments to evaluate the effectiveness of personalized NLP models, exploring and
analyzing data, collaborating on the creation and curation of annotated datasets, and
writing scientific papers.

Successful candidates will need to demonstrate:
-  proven experience in Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a focus on subjective tasks and model personalization.
- programming skills in languages such as Python and expertise in the use of NLP frameworks such as PyTorch.
- experience in publishing papers in leading conferences in the field of computational linguistics.

Applicants should attach to their application a list of publications and a motivation letter.

### Elegibility 
In order to participate in the competition, Candidates for the position as Post-Doc
Researcher must be scholars with a PhD or equivalent qualification, obtained in Italy or
abroad, or in the process of completing their PhD within six months from the date of
publication of this call, with the exclusion of Professors and researchers who already hold
a position with tenure, even if they have ceased service.

Applicants should apply by submitting the complete package via our on-line application
system, which can be found at https://jobmarket.unibocconi.eu/?id=1004 by 23:59 of 13th
November 2026 (Italian time).

Each candidate’s application must clearly indicate:
- surname and name
- date and place of birth
- citizenship
- residence
- contact address 

The application must include the following documents, which must be attached to the application:
- professional C.V., including a list of any titles/qualifications and publications, in pdf format;
- any publication, or document that the candidate intends to submit for evaluation, in pdf format, up to a maximum of 10 scientific publications;
- a motivation letter

For any problem related to uploading files, the candidate can contact faculty@unibocconi.it
